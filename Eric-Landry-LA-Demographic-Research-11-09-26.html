Demographic profile of the Louisiana parishes where your leads and your office sit: household income, homeownership, home values, age and commuting, built from the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey five-year estimates (QuickFacts, 2019 to 2023 and 2020 to 2024 vintages as published for each parish) and, for the Houma and Thibodaux ZIP codes, the ZIP-level ACS 2020 to 2024 figures. Your 128 GOAL Auto leads since October 14 come from 35 parishes and 85 ZIP codes across Louisiana, so this report leads with the largest lead parishes, East Baton Rouge (23 leads), Ouachita (11), Tangipahoa (7), Caddo (7) and Ascension (6), and with your home market, Terrebonne (4) and Lafourche (5). The nine parishes profiled hold 72 of the 128 leads (56.2%) and 657,920 households, 36.5% of Louisiana. Your Houma office sits in Terrebonne Parish, and only 9 leads (7.0%) come from Terrebonne and Lafourche, so most of the market described here is one your team serves by phone. This edition profiles parishes rather than every Louisiana ZIP; the companion spreadsheet carries every lead ZIP with its parish profile, and a full ZIP-by-ZIP table can follow. The hurricane and flood report covers the risk geography.

Household profile

Age, household size, commuting and education · your nine profiled parishes

Age and household size
  • OldestCaddo 19.6% of residents are 65 or over, above Louisiana at 18.8%; Rapides, Ouachita, Lafourche and East Baton Rouge sit between 17% and 18%
  • YoungestAscension 13.7% and Lafayette and Tangipahoa at about 15% to 16%: families, teen drivers and first homes
  • Household sizeAscension has the largest households (2.73 people) and Caddo the smallest (2.40); Louisiana is 2.49
  • Why it mattersOlder, owner-heavy parishes lean toward Home and fewer vehicles per household; younger family parishes lean toward multi-car Auto and a first-home bundle.
Commute and education
  • Longest commutesAscension 29.9 minutes, Tangipahoa 29.5 and Lafourche 26.5: more miles on the road, which is an Auto conversation about annual mileage
  • ShortestCaddo 20.6 minutes and Ouachita 22.1; Louisiana averages 25.7
  • EducationBachelor's degree or higher runs from 17.7% in Terrebonne to 38.5% in East Baton Rouge; Louisiana is 27.0%
  • Poverty10.7% in Ascension to 22.8% in Caddo; Louisiana is 18.7%. Price-conscious shoppers are likely in the northern lead parishes and East Baton Rouge.
